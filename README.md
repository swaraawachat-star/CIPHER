# CIPHER – Mechanical Puzzle Box

**CAD Software:** Onshape  
**Project Type:** Mechanical Engineering / Interactive Puzzle  
**Development Status:** Concept Development and Initial Mechanical Design

## About the Project

CIPHER is an interactive mechanical puzzle box designed around a sequence of movements that the player must figure out through trial and error. The objective is to unlock a concealed compartment by correctly interacting with a series of mechanical components.

Unlike a conventional combination lock, CIPHER is intended to involve multiple stages of interaction. The player must experiment with rotating discs, understand how the locking mechanism responds and discover the sequence required to release the compartment.

The project focuses on mechanical design, component interaction, motion constraints and the development of a functional puzzle mechanism. The entire design is being developed in Onshape.

## How It Works

The proposed mechanism consists of three main stages:

1. **Rotating Disc Mechanism:** The player rotates three independent discs, attempting to align their internal notches with a common locking bar.
2. **Sliding Mechanism:** Once the discs are correctly aligned, the locking bar can move, allowing a horizontal slider to travel.
3. **Secondary Release:** Moving the slider reveals a secondary lever. Operating this lever releases the compartment latch, allowing the concealed compartment to open.

The puzzle is designed around trial and error rather than simply presenting the player with a visible combination.

## Proposed Components

- Three independently rotating discs
- Disc axles and spacers
- Common locking bar
- Locking bar guide rails
- Horizontal sliding mechanism
- Secondary release lever
- Compartment latch
- Sliding compartment
- Main enclosure
- Removable top cover
- Fasteners and connecting components

## Design Objectives

- Develop a mechanical puzzle with multiple stages of interaction.
- Ensure that all rotating components operate independently.
- Design a locking mechanism that responds to the alignment of all three discs.
- Incorporate sliding and rotational movements into one assembly.
- Minimise interference between moving components.
- Make the puzzle reusable by incorporating a reset sequence.
- Create a complete CAD assembly with movable components.

## Software and Tools

- Onshape for 3D modelling and assembly.
- CAD sketches for planning component geometry.
- Assembly mates for defining component movement.
- Interference checks for identifying collisions between parts.

## Development Approach

The project will be developed in stages, beginning with the central rotating disc and locking bar mechanism. Once the interaction between these components is established, the sliding mechanism and secondary release will be designed.

The individual components will then be assembled in Onshape, where their movement, clearances and possible interference will be evaluated. The external enclosure will be designed after the internal mechanism is sufficiently developed.

## Current Progress

The initial concept, puzzle rules and proposed mechanism have been outlined. The main components and their intended functions have also been identified.

The next stage involves developing the geometry of the rotating discs and figuring out how the common locking bar will interact with them.

## Project Goal

The final goal is to create a mechanically functional puzzle box that combines multiple types of motion into a single interactive system. CIPHER will serve as an exploration of mechanical engineering, CAD modelling and practical problem-solving.
